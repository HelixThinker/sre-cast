# SRE CAST

SRE CAST is an open toolkit for systems-based analysis of reliability incidents
with AI assistance.

The site will provide:

- a practical introduction to CAST for SRE practitioners;
- reusable AI rules and guided skills;
- downloadable analysis templates;
- visual, evidence-linked worked examples;
- references to the source books behind methodological decisions.

## Project areas

- [AI rules](https://github.com/BogdanDolia/sre-cast/tree/main/rules)
- [AI skills](https://github.com/BogdanDolia/sre-cast/tree/main/skills)
- [Templates](https://github.com/BogdanDolia/sre-cast/tree/main/templates)
- [Worked examples](https://github.com/BogdanDolia/sre-cast/tree/main/examples)
- [Source repository](https://github.com/BogdanDolia/sre-cast)

Worked examples will remain canonical under `examples/`. The website will present
them without maintaining a second, divergent copy of the analysis.

CAST reports should make relationships among causal factors understandable and
may use hyperlinks to connect the analysis. See the
[CAST Handbook, p. 92](https://psas.scripts.mit.edu/home/get_file4.php?name=CAST_Handbook.pdf#page=92).
